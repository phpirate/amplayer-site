AmPlayer Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

This policy explains how the AmPlayer app for Android ("AmPlayer", "the app") handles your information. AmPlayer is developed by an independent developer ("I", "me").

In short: AmPlayer does not collect, upload or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your music, playlists and settings stay on your phone.

Information that stays on your device

To work as a music player, AmPlayer reads and stores the following, only on your device. None of it is sent to me or to anyone else.

  • Your music library: the audio files on your device and their details (title, artist, album, duration, album art), so you can browse and play them.
  • Your playlists and favourites.
  • Your sound settings: volume boost, equalizer, saved EQ presets, speed and pitch.
  • Headphone profiles: the name of each headphone or speaker you play through (for example the Bluetooth device name), so AmPlayer can restore your settings when you connect it again.
  • Whether you've unlocked AmPlayer Pro.

This information is deleted when you uninstall AmPlayer or clear its data in Android settings.

Permissions

PermissionWhy AmPlayer asks for it
Music and audioTo find and play the audio files on your device.
Photos and imagesTo show album art. Android requires this alongside music access on some versions. AmPlayer does not look at your photos.
NotificationsTo show playback controls while music plays.
Background playbackTo keep music playing when the screen is off or you're in another app.
Audio settingsTo apply the volume boost and the equalizer.
Modify a song file (asked each time)Only when you edit a song's title, artist or album: Android asks whether AmPlayer may change that one file, and the new details are saved into it. On Android 9 and older, this is the storage permission.
Google Play billingTo let you buy AmPlayer Pro.

Purchases

AmPlayer Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. I never see your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns Pro, so it can unlock Pro features and restore them after a reinstall.

To process purchases, Google Play's billing service on your device communicates with Google, and it may send Google diagnostic information about the purchase process. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Apart from this, AmPlayer itself does not connect to the internet.

Sharing

I don't sell, rent or share any information about you, because AmPlayer does not collect any.

Children

AmPlayer is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
